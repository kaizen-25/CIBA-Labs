Who We Are

About CIBA Pharmaceuticals

CIBA Pharma is a science-led pharmaceutical company focused on expanding access to high-quality, affordable medicines across global markets.

Company Overview

CIBA Pharmaceuticals partners with leading manufacturers, healthcare institutions, and distributors to develop and deliver a diversified portfolio of essential and specialty medicines. With a disciplined focus on quality, compliance, and market insight, the organization is structured to support long-term, sustainable growth in both emerging and mature healthcare markets.

Our Story

Founded by a team of clinicians, scientists, and supply chain experts, CIBA Pharma was created to address the persistent disparity in medicine availability between regions.

Over the years, we have built a diversified portfolio of essential and specialty medicines, strengthened our regulatory footprint, and established long-term partnerships with hospitals, distributors, and government agencies.
